# Partner with TMU BattleBots

TMU BattleBots is a student-led combat robotics team focused on engineering, competition, and hands-on design. We build robots that force us to solve real mechanical, electrical, and software problems under pressure, and that experience carries directly into industry.

By sponsoring TMU BattleBots, you support that work while gaining meaningful visibility with a community of students, faculty, competitors, and industry contacts.


## Why Sponsor Us?

- Access to emerging engineering talent from across multiple disciplines.
- Brand visibility on our website, social posts, demo tables, and competition material.
- A strong student-led team identity tied to practical engineering and competitive robotics.
- Direct engagement with TMU students and the broader robotics community.


## What Sponsors Can Expect:

- Logo placement on the TMU BattleBots website.
- Recognition at club fairs, showcases, and competition booths.
- Public acknowledgement through team presentations and outreach.
- A partnership with a focused, motivated engineering team.

## Partnership Levels

Our sponsorship package is designed to be flexible. Whether you are a local business, alumni group, or industry partner, we can tailor recognition to fit your support level and visibility goals. Here are some of the partner tiers that we offer:

### Community partners: 
Support the team at an accessible level and help fund materials, outreach, and competition preparation.

### Supporting partners

Gain stronger brand placement and a visible presence across our website and event material.

### Major partners

Receive prominent acknowledgement across the season and on the team assets we bring to public events.

### Presenting partners

For sponsors looking for the highest level of recognition and a closer relationship with the team.

## Where your brand appears

Competitions: TMU BattleBots participates in BotBrawl Canada’s annual competition, giving sponsors exposure to students, competitors, and industry professionals.

Fairs and showcases: We use club fairs, demos, and presentations to reach TMU students and faculty.

Website: Sponsor logos are featured on the home page and sponsor page for ongoing visibility.

## Let’s build something together; We are always eager to connect with sponsors that share our passion for engineering, innovation, hands-on learning, and competitive robotics.
